How to setup a missile

1. Use schematics to paste the missile in the world. Note: No extra blocks should be attrached to the missile
2. Shift right click the ship helm and assemble the missile
3. Open the command computer, type "lua" and press enter. Then type "ship.setScaleI(0.5)" and press enter to scale the missile
4. Destroy the advanced computer, and replace it with the computer with the correct ID (The computer with the script installed).
5. Connect the computer to the electric motor with wired modem, right click the modem if the red signal light isn't Open
6. Connect the computer with Ender modem
7. Place a redstone transmitor on the left or the right side of the computer. This will be the detonation transmitor, make them the same channel as those transmitor on the bombs
    BEWARE!!!: the restone transmition channel on every missile must be different
8. Start the missileOnboard.lua on the computer, type in your desired missile number (Better if you have them in accending order starting from 1)
9. break any extra blocks, including the command computer and the ship helm
10. right click the propeller bearing to assemble it

11. Paste / build the control system. Make them a ship(phyiscal object)
12. Start the missHubGen2 script on the computer

13. Use A and D to scroll through each missile. The missile nubmer will be shown. Note: controls are done on the tweaked controller
14. Use W and S to scroll through the detected target, the currently locked target will be highlighted in blue.
15. Press Enter to launch and press mouse left click to cancel launch

Remark: There will not be any hit confirmation
